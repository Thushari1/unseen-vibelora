## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies wonder, enchanting storytelling, and high-fidelity creative imagination. Designed for digital natives, modern animation enthusiasts, and creators seeking joy, the interface feels like entering an interactive twilight theater where magic and animation converge. 

The visual design language is an expressive hybrid of **tactile dreamscapes and atmospheric glassmorphism**, accented with Pixar-grade luminous lighting. Key characteristics include:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered midnight-violet cosmos balanced with ethereal star dust and soft nebular backdrops.
- **Luminescent Tactility:** Interactive elements carry an inner luminescence—appearing illuminated from within rather than resting statically on the surface.
- **Whimsical Organic Geometry:** Expansive radii, friendly capsules, and organic curves evoke comfort, warmth, and cinematic approachability.
- **Magic Accents:** Delicate 4-point star sparkles (`✦`), micro-auroras, and dimensional gradients bring static screens to life without compromising content hierarchy.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in the magic hour between twilight and deep cosmic space, illuminated by playful neon-candy accents and warm celestial firelight.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#FF70A6` / Star Pink):** Used for primary conversion triggers, hero badges, interactive play states, and luminous focal glow effects.
- **Secondary (`#C8B6FF` / Cosmic Lavender):** Powers secondary interactive components, delicate borders, selected indicators, and ethereal metadata tags.
- **Tertiary (`#FFD166` / Star Gold):** Reserved for stellar accomplishments, ratings, premium tiers, animated sparkles, and energetic highlights. Supported by `#F4A261` (Sun-flare amber) for expressive gradients.
- **Neutral Base (`#1A0B2E` / Nightfall Velvet):** The deep midnight canvas. Supported by surface step-ups: `#23103D` (Surface Low), `#28124D` (Surface Base), and `#381C66` (Surface High).

### Tinted Transparencies & Glows
- **Luminous Edge Strokes:** Semi-transparent borders set to `rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.2)` on resting containers, stepping up to `rgba(255, 112, 166, 0.45)` on active or focused states.
- **Ethereal Glow Fill:** Gradients transitioning from `#FF70A6` at 18% opacity to `#8C6BAE` at 0% opacity for floating cards and background nebulas.
- **Content Legibility:** Text primary sits at `#FFFFFF` (solid pure white) and text muted sits at `#C8B6FF` (70% opacity) to maintain maximum contrast over dark violet planes.

## Typography
Typography is architected around the contrast between playful, organic warmth and high-legibility clarity:

- **Headlines & Display (Quicksand):** Rounded, animated geometry with generous counters and soft terminals. Imparts a friendly storybook voice while maintaining high craft. Used across screen headers, character naming, story titles, and hero value statements.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A modern, geometric sans-serif featuring subtle warm humanist curves that harmonizes with Quicksand while ensuring effortless readability during extended reading, caption parsing, and dense AI prompt drafting.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display types must preserve negative tracking to retain visual density. Do not uppercase Quicksand headings; use Title Case or Sentence Case to retain friendly whimsy.

## Layout & Spacing
A 4-column layout is utilized for mobile viewports (`< 600px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout for tablets (`600px - 1023px`), and a 12-column layout for desktop environments (`>= 1024px`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Mobile First:** The canvas defaults to vertical storytelling stacks with edge-snapping horizontal carousels (peeking out by `24px` to indicate discoverable overflow).
- **Margins & Gutters:** Base mobile margin is locked at `1rem` (16px) with an inner column gutter of `0.75rem` (12px). On desktop screens, maximum layout width is constrained to `1200px` centered with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px modular unit. Fluid micro-gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) are strictly reserved for badge insets and title-to-subtitle relationships, while generous macro-gaps (`space-xl`) isolate distinct story episodes or character deck groupings.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids sterile drop shadows in favor of a **Luminescent Layering System** that simulates glowing surfaces floating in space.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid deep violet `#1A0B2E`. Static underlying universe with subtle radial gradients of `#28124D` at 60% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Submerged Containers):** `#23103D` at 70% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, encircled with a subtle 1px border of `rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Active Modules):** `#28124D` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Rim-lit along the top border with a 1px gradient: `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(255, 112, 166, 0.4), rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.2))`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(10, 3, 20, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Action Sheets, Floating Controls):** Gradient fill from `#351866` to `#28124D` with an exterior magical bloom: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -8px rgba(12, 4, 28, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(255, 112, 166, 0.25)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy is built on pill geometry and welcoming, organic hyper-radii (Level 3: Pill-shaped).

- **Pill Philosophy:** Badges, chips, action buttons, progress bars, and navigation segments use full pill radii (`rounded-full` / `9999px`). This eliminates visual sharpness and supports the animated, cartoonish tactility.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Medium-to-large containers implement `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px), creating pebble-like frames reminiscent of high-end toy design and animation studio interfaces.
- **Asymmetric Whimsical Accents:** Special hero cards and episodic frames may apply asymmetric radii (e.g., `rounded-tl-3xl rounded-br-3xl rounded-tr-lg rounded-bl-lg`) to evoke classic storybook pages.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Magic Button:** Pill-shaped, gradient-filled (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FF70A6, #FFAFCC)`). Text in white with subtle text shadow. Box shadow provides a soft pink ambient bloom (`0 4px 20px rgba(255, 112, 166, 0.4)`). Active state scales to `0.97` with an intensified starburst highlight.
- **Secondary Cosmic Button:** Pill-shaped, frosted dark glass (`rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.1)`), rimmed with a 1.5px border of `#C8B6FF` at 40% opacity. Text in `#C8B6FF`.
- **Icon Buttons:** Circular (`44x44px`), glass base with embedded golden star sparkles or animated glyphs centered.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Height 28px, padded `12px` horizontally. Cosmic background (`rgba(40, 18, 77, 0.8)`), border in `rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.3)`. Text in `label-sm` or `label-md`.
- **Special Tier / AI Sparkle Badge:** Tertiary Star Gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFD166, #F4A261)`) with purple text (`#1A0B2E`), prefixed with an inline 4-point star SVG (`✦`).

### Cards & Story Deck Modules
- **Artwork & Content Cards:** 28px border-radius. Top-lit gradient borders. Inner padding set to `space-md` on mobile, `space-lg` on tablet/desktop. Image backdrops utilize rounded inner containers (`20px`) with a soft vignette merging into the card's violet floor.

### Input Fields
- **Prompt & Creation Bars:** Pill-shaped container filled with `#23103D`, framed in a 1.5px border of `rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.2)`. On focus, the border shifts to `#FF70A6` accompanied by an ethereal outer aura (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 112, 166, 0.3)`). Placeholder text rendered in muted cosmic lavender `#8C6BAE`.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Toggles
- **Toggles:** Pill track with deep violet base `#23103D`. Active track illuminates in Star Pink `#FF70A6`. Thumb is a floating star-white sphere containing an embossed star micro-glyph.
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded-md squares (8px radius). Checked state transitions into a solid `#FF70A6` surface with a crisp white checkmark.

### Distinctive Domain Components
- **Magic Prompt Generator Pill:** Floating bottom-anchored input bar crowned with a gradient border, featuring an embedded "Generate" wand button that pulses with soft Star Gold starlight.
- **Character Avatar Portal:** Circular avatar frames surrounded by a glowing dual-tone orbital ring (`#FF70A6` and `#FFD166`) that rotates slowly during AI cartoon generation states.
